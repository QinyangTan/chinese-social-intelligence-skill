## Inspiration and attribution

This project was inspired in part by the engineering direction of:

- SocialAI-tianji/Tianji — https://github.com/SocialAI-tianji/Tianji
- Tianji is licensed under the Apache License 2.0.

Tianji demonstrates organizing Chinese social-interaction capabilities around scenarios such as etiquette, hospitality, gifting, wishes, communication, awkward situations, and conflict.

This repository does **not** vendor Tianji model weights, datasets, source code, or prose. Its rule texts, decision framework, examples, and evaluation cases are independently written and are intended to transform cultural and social knowledge into a portable Agent Skill.

## Classical sources

Short classical phrases and principles referenced in this repository come from public-domain classical Chinese works. Modern interpretations and executable rules are original summaries; see SOURCES.md.

## Modern sources

Modern public materials are cited as source context. This repository summarizes behavioral ideas rather than reproducing copyrighted textbook chapters.
